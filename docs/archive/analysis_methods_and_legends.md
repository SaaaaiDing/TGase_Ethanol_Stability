# 1IU4–ZQG MD comparison: methods and figure legends

## Data and sampling

Three independent *conditions* were examined: water, approximately 20% v/v ethanol, and approximately 50% v/v ethanol. Each condition has one 200 ns Desmond trajectory. Pair energies were recalculated on 201 saved frames per trajectory (every fifth trajectory frame, approximately 1 ns apart), using OPLS 2005 explicit-solvent energy groups. The same sampled frame indices were used for reactive geometry and the Cys64 solvent shell. Time 0–50 ns comprises 51 sampled frames; 50–200 ns comprises 150 sampled frames. There are no independent trajectory replicates. Frames within a trajectory are correlated; frame SDs and frame counts are descriptive and are not experimental uncertainty or independent-sample statistics.

The reactive-near state requires Cys64 SG–ZQG C28 ≤5 Å using minimum-image periodic distances. This geometric criterion does not establish a reaction-ready pose. The Cys64 backbone NH–ZQG O22 hydrogen-bond proxy requires N···O ≤3.5 Å and N–H···O ≥120°. The six-angstrom solvent shell counts water oxygen atoms and ethanol hydroxyl oxygen atoms within 6 Å of Cys64 SG. The local ethanol oxygen fraction is the sum of local ethanol oxygen counts divided by the sum of local ethanol and water oxygen counts over the indicated sampled frames. The whole-system reference is the ethanol molecule-number fraction among ethanol and water molecules: 0.0704 for 20% and 0.2234 for 50%. These number fractions are not ethanol volume fractions. The local shell calculation alone is not a preferential-solvation free energy or a corrected preferential-interaction coefficient.

Desmond energy-group values are electrostatic plus van der Waals pair-interaction terms between the indicated residue and ZQG. They are not residue MM/GBSA contributions, binding free energies, activation barriers, or rate constants. Electrostatic terms from Arg208 and Arg238 can be substantial even without a direct short-range contact. In the 20% trajectory late-far state, mean Arg208–ZQG and Arg238–ZQG electrostatic terms are −76.26 and −91.08 kcal/mol, whereas the corresponding mean van der Waals terms are +1.60 and +0.35 kcal/mol. Interpret these terms with the earlier contact maps and 3D conformations.

## Figure 2. Residue–ZQG interaction signatures

Heatmap of mean residue–ZQG electrostatic plus van der Waals pair-interaction energies among reactive-near frames. The early window includes all three solvent conditions; the late window includes water and 50% ethanol. The 20% ethanol late-near condition (n=4 sampled frames) is omitted. Numbers in the cells are kcal/mol and column headers give the sampled frame counts. Negative values indicate favorable pair-interaction terms within the specified force field, but cannot be added or interpreted as a binding free-energy decomposition. Asp255 and other charged-residue terms are especially sensitive to the electrostatic environment. The water and 50% ethanol late-near populations have different interaction signatures and carbonyl hydrogen-bond geometry; direct comparison does not establish a difference in catalytic efficiency.

## Tables

`1IU4_state_and_solvent_comparison.csv` reports state occupancies, Cys64 NH–O22 geometry, median reactive-carbon distance, local solvent counts, local and global ethanol oxygen-number fractions, and sample-size flags. `1IU4_residue_pair_energy_comparison.csv` reports mean electrostatic, van der Waals, and total terms plus median and frame SD for each residue by system, time window, and proximity state. Rows with fewer than ten sampled frames are flagged. No significance tests or confidence intervals are assigned from the single correlated trajectory per condition.

## Interpretation supported by these data

In the 50–200 ns window, the sampled reactive-near fractions are 73/150 (water), 4/150 (20% ethanol), and 136/150 (50% ethanol). The corresponding Cys64 NH–O22 proxy counts within reactive-near frames are 65/73, 0/4, and 0/136. Thus, the high substrate residence near Cys64 in 50% ethanol does not demonstrate a productive catalytic orientation. For the 50% ethanol late-near frames, the local ethanol oxygen fraction at Cys64 is 0.079, below the whole-system molecule-number fraction of 0.223. In the 20% ethanol late-far frames, the local fraction is 0.136, above its whole-system fraction of 0.070. These are state-conditioned descriptions of these trajectories, not evidence that ethanol improves enzyme activity.
