Figure 1. Protein conformational dynamics and ZQG positioning in aqueous ethanol.



(a) Protein Cα root-mean-square deviation (RMSD) relative to the reference structure after protein alignment. (b) RMSD of ZQG heavy atoms after applying the protein alignment. (c) Minimum distance between Cys64 Sγ (SG) and any ZQG heavy atom. Blue, orange, and green denote water, 20% (v/v) ethanol–water, and 50% (v/v) ethanol–water, respectively. Vertical dashed lines mark 50 ns, which separates the early and late analysis windows. Each trace represents one 200-ns trajectory. ***The distance in (c) measures general peptide proximity to Cys64 and is distinct from the SG–C28 reaction-center distance examined in Figure 3.***





Figure 2. Residue-resolved flexibility of microbial transglutaminase in aqueous ethanol.



Protein Cα root-mean-square fluctuation (RMSF) calculated over 50–200 ns for water (blue), 20% (v/v) ethanol–water (orange), and 50% (v/v) ethanol–water (green). Protein coordinates were fitted by least squares using all 331 Cα atoms, with three iterations of alignment to the mean configuration of the analysis window. RMSF was then calculated for each residue relative to its mean aligned position. ***The horizontal axis gives the chain-A residue number, and the vertical axis gives RMSF in Å. Each profile was obtained from 751 frames of one trajectory per condition; the curves describe spatial fluctuations within those sampled trajectories.***





Figure 3. Geometry of the Cys64–ZQG reactive-atom pair.



(a, c, e) Time-dependent distance between the sulfur atom of Cys64, Sγ (SG), and the glutamine side-chain carboxamide carbonyl carbon of ZQG, C28, in water, 20% (v/v) ethanol–water, and 50% (v/v) ethanol–water, respectively. Horizontal dashed lines mark the 5 Å proximity threshold, and vertical dotted lines mark 50 ns. Percentages in the upper-right corners indicate the fractions of all 751 frames from 50–200 ns satisfying SG–C28 ≤5 Å: 49.0%, 2.9%, and 92.5%, respectively. ***The distance panels use different vertical-axis ranges.*** (b, d, f) Normalized distributions of the O22–C28–SG angle for the corresponding conditions, restricted to 50–200 ns frames satisfying SG–C28 ≤5 Å. O22 is the oxygen bonded to carbonyl carbon C28. Histograms use 5° bins, and black vertical lines indicate medians. The angle distributions contain 368, 22, and 695 frames, respectively. ***The distance threshold and angular distributions describe the sampled geometry of the unreacted enzyme–substrate complex; they do not establish formation of a covalent intermediate or determine a catalytic rate.***





Figure 4. ZQG proximity, carbonyl hydrogen bonding, and local solvation at Cys64.



(a) Cys64 SG–ZQG C28 distance across 200 ns. Pale points show frames sampled at every fifth saved frame***, approximately 1 ns apart***; solid lines show a centered nine-sample rolling median. The horizontal dashed line marks 5 Å, and the vertical dotted line marks 50 ns. (b) Percentage of sampled frames satisfying SG–C28 ≤5 Å in the early (0–50 ns) and late (50–200 ns) windows. There are 51 and 150 sampled frames in these windows, respectively; numbers above bars are the counts satisfying the distance criterion. (c) Percentage of those proximal frames that also satisfy the Cys64 backbone N–H···ZQG O22 hydrogen-bond criterion: N···O ≤3.5 Å and N–H···O ≥120°. The orange cross identifies a subset containing only four proximal frames, for which a percentage bar is omitted***; it does not represent zero hydrogen-bond occupancy***. (d) Local ethanol oxygen number fraction within 6 Å of Cys64 SG (filled circles), compared with the whole-system solvent oxygen number fraction (open diamonds). Local fractions were calculated as the summed ethanol oxygen counts divided by the summed ethanol-plus-water oxygen counts over each indicated subset. Connecting lines indicate the difference between local and whole-system composition, rather than uncertainty. “Early” and “late” refer to the two analysis windows; “near” and “far” denote SG–C28 ≤5 Å and >5 Å, respectively. Blue, orange, and green identify water, 20% ethanol, and 50% ethanol throughout. Local oxygen number fractions are distinct from the nominal volume fractions used to prepare the mixtures.





Figure 5. Residue-specific nonbonded interactions with ZQG in proximal configurations.



Mean electrostatic-plus-van der Waals interaction energy between each indicated enzyme residue and ZQG, calculated using the simulation force field and restricted to sampled frames with Cys64 SG–ZQG C28 ≤5 Å. Rows identify residues, and columns identify solvent conditions and time windows. Columns from left to right contain 38, 45, 49, 73, and 136 proximal frames, respectively, from the same every-fifth-frame sampling used in Figure 4. Cell values are means in kcal mol−1. Blue and red indicate negative and positive interaction energies on a common scale from −20 to +20 kcal mol−1. The 20% ethanol subset from 50–200 ns contains only four proximal sampled frames and is omitted. ***These quantities are direct force-field interaction terms; they are not MM/GBSA binding free energies or residue contributions to a binding free energy. Different sample counts reflect the observed proximity populations and do not represent independent simulation replicates.***





Figure 6. Representative enzyme–ZQG configurations and atomic contacts.



(a–c) Overall enzyme–ZQG configurations in water, 20% (v/v) ethanol–water, and 50% (v/v) ethanol–water, respectively. (d–f) Corresponding local views of the selected atomic contacts. The water configuration (a, d; 116.0 ns) was selected from the late proximal subset with a backbone NH–O22 contact. ***It shows Cys64 N···ZQG O22 and Tyr75 OH···ZQG O24 donor–acceptor distances of 2.73 and 2.74 Å, respectively, and a Cys64 SG···ZQG C28 distance of 3.47 Å.*** The 20% ethanol configuration (b, e; 125.4 ns) was selected from the late displaced subset and shows Arg208 NH2···ZQG O24 and Arg238 NH2···ZQG O21 distances of 2.77 and 2.58 Å, respectively***; the SG···C28 distance is 12.70 Å***. The 50% ethanol configuration (c, f; 141.8 ns) was selected from the late proximal subset ***and shows Asn253 ND2···ZQG O24 and ZQG N36···Asn276 backbone O distances of 2.68 and 3.06 Å, respectively; the SG···C28 distance is 4.45 Å***. The enzyme is shown as a gray cartoon, selected residues as sticks, and ZQG as sticks with cyan carbon atoms. Orange dashed lines denote SG···C28 distances; the other colored dashed lines identify the selected polar contacts. Contact distances are heavy-atom distances in individual snapshots. Polar contacts were screened using a donor–acceptor distance ≤3.5 Å and a donor–hydrogen–acceptor angle ≥120°. ***The snapshots illustrate different sampled states and are not estimates of their population or binding affinity.***
